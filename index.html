Campaign groups: EZ = names containing "EZ", BO = names ending in "BO", Other = everything else. Priority: urgent = $100+ monthly spend on the row (or 6+ orders for a new keyword), medium = $30–100, small = under $30. Break-even (BE) = the ACOS at which a sale earns $0 after COGS and Amazon fees, from Sellerboard, per keyword where Sellerboard has it. Lower bid = keyword ACOS above its break-even and current bid more than 15% above Sellerboard's break-even bid; new bid = 90% of break-even bid. Pause / negative = zero orders with at least 15 clicks (about a 10% chance of happening by luck at the account's 14% conversion rate), or a Nespresso/Vertuo search, which PeleTrade has no product for. New exact keyword = 3+ orders at or under break-even and not yet a keyword anywhere. Every number was re-checked against the original Sellerboard export.
