Before changing any bid
  1. Open the campaign's settings and check the bidding strategy and placement modifiers. In this account you pay on median 1.24× your bid per click, and 1.68× in M19 campaigns, so something is raising bids. Set "Dynamic bids – down only" (or fixed) and placement modifiers to 0% where you are cutting, or the new bids will land higher than planned.
  2. Bids move at most 25% down (15% when the loss is moderate) or 20–30% up per step. Wait 14 days, look at the next export, then take the next step.
  3. The last 7 days of any export are still filling in with orders (Amazon credits orders up to 7 days after the click), so recent zeros are partly not final.

Campaign groups: EZ = names containing "EZ", BO = names ending in "BO", Other = everything else. Priority: urgent = $100+ monthly spend on the row (or 6+ orders for a new keyword), medium = $30–100, small = under $30. Break-even (BE) = the ACOS at which a sale earns $0 after COGS, Amazon fees and refunds. Cuts are judged against the HIGHER of Sellerboard's per-keyword break-even and the product's own margin (Sellerboard product report), so nothing profitable gets cut; raises, new keywords and budgets use the LOWER of the two, so nothing is pushed into a loss. Lower bid = ACOS more than 10% above break-even; target CPC = current CPC × break-even ÷ ACOS, less a 20% safety margin under 30 orders, translated through the keyword's own CPC-to-bid ratio, at most −25% per step. Keyword ACOS 1.1–1.5× break-even = keep as is. 1.5–2× = −15%. 2× or more = −25%. Raise bid = 5+ orders and ACOS under half of break-even: +20%, or +30% when under 35% of break-even with 10+ orders. Pause / negative = zero orders in 31 days with at least 10 clicks and at least 2.5 ÷ the campaign's own conversion rate, and more spend than one order is worth at break-even; or a Nespresso/Vertuo search, which PeleTrade has no product for. Anything that ever sold is never blocked: it gets a bid change or moves to exact. New exact keyword = 3+ orders at or under break-even and not yet a keyword anywhere.
